Lore is free during its beta. Retrieval can be incomplete, and client/model behavior varies. Windows beta binaries and scripts are unsigned; macOS builds are unsigned/unnotarized previews. Check OS and client compatibility before relying on the app.

Browser preview

The extension is an unpacked developer preview. Store publishing and authenticated live-site validation are pending. It captures recognized visible messages rather than complete provider account histories. Review retrieved context before sending it to another AI service.
